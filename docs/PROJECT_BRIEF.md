# Project Brief / 项目简报

## Background / 项目背景

International students often need to manage many practical details after moving abroad: banking, healthcare registration, housing documents, travel files, budgets and emergency contacts. These tasks are usually spread across notes, screenshots, email attachments and chat messages.

海外留学生到达新国家后，经常需要同时处理很多生活细节：银行开户、医疗注册、住房文件、旅行材料、预算记录和紧急联系方式。这些信息往往分散在备忘录、截图、邮件附件和聊天记录里。

Study Abroad Life Toolkit is planned as a small mobile app prototype to keep these records in one local place.

海外留学生活助手计划做成一个小型手机端 App 原型，用一个本地工具集中整理这些记录。

## Target Users / 目标用户

- International students preparing to move abroad.
- Students who have recently arrived in a new country.
- Students who want a simple local checklist and document record.
- First version focus: students living or studying in the UK.

- 准备出国或刚到海外的留学生。
- 需要整理生活事项、文件和旅行材料的学生。
- 希望用一个本地工具记录 checklist、文件和预算的人。
- 第一版重点场景：英国留学生。

## Problem / 解决的问题

The app helps users answer practical questions such as:

- Which life tasks are still unfinished?
- Which important documents have been prepared?
- What files are needed for an upcoming trip?
- How much of this month's budget has been used?
- Where are emergency phone numbers stored?

这个 App 希望帮助用户快速回答这些问题：

- 哪些生活事项还没完成？
- 哪些重要文件还没准备好？
- 下一次旅行需要哪些材料？
- 本月预算已经用了多少？
- 紧急联系电话在哪里？

## Project Boundary / 项目边界

The app is a local organisation tool. It does not provide official information, legal judgement or professional advice.

这个项目只是本地整理工具，不提供官方信息判断，也不提供专业建议。

It does not provide:

- Visa advice.
- Legal advice.
- Medical advice.
- Real-time official policy updates.
- Booking, banking or healthcare services.

它不提供：

- 签证建议。
- 法律建议。
- 医疗建议。
- 实时官方政策更新。
- 预订、银行或医疗服务。

## First-Version Scope / 第一版范围

The first version is planned to include:

- Home dashboard.
- Life checklist.
- Document wallet.
- Travel wallet.
- Budget tracker.
- Emergency contacts.
- Search.
- English and Chinese UI language support.
- Local-only storage.

第一版计划包含：

- 首页总览。
- 生活事项清单。
- 重要文件夹。
- 旅行材料管理。
- 生活预算记录。
- 紧急联系方式。
- 全局搜索。
- 中英文界面。
- 本地保存。
