# Project Brief / 项目说明

## Project Identity / 项目名称

中文名称：海外留学生活助手

English name: Study Abroad Life Toolkit

Suggested GitHub repository name: `study-abroad-life-toolkit`

## Project Background / 项目背景

International students often need to manage many small but important life details after preparing to study abroad or arriving in a new country. These may include opening a bank account, registering with a local doctor, saving university contacts, preparing tenancy documents, keeping travel materials, and tracking basic expenses.

This information is often scattered across emails, notes, screenshots, cloud drives, PDFs, and chat messages. Study Abroad Life Toolkit explores a simple mobile app prototype that keeps these records organized locally.

留学生在准备出国和刚到海外生活时，通常需要同时处理很多零散但重要的事情，比如开通银行账户、注册 GP 或本地医生、保存学校联系方式、整理租房文件、保存旅行材料和记录日常开销。

这些信息经常分散在邮件、备忘录、截图、网盘、PDF 和聊天记录里。海外留学生活助手希望先以手机端原型的方式，探索一个更清楚的本地整理工具。

## Target Users / 目标用户

Primary users:

- International students preparing to study abroad.
- International students who have recently arrived in the United Kingdom.
- Students who want a local tool for documents, checklists, travel materials, budgets, and contacts.

Future users may include students in other countries or regions if country-specific default checklists are added later.

主要目标用户：

- 正在准备海外留学的学生。
- 刚到英国生活和学习的留学生。
- 希望用一个本地工具整理文件、清单、旅行材料、预算和联系方式的学生。

如果后续加入不同国家或地区的默认内容，也可以扩展给更多地区的留学生使用。

## Problem To Solve / 要解决的问题

The app should help users:

- See important preparation status at a glance.
- Track common life tasks.
- Keep document records and notes together.
- Save travel plans and travel files.
- Track simple monthly spending.
- Find emergency contacts quickly.
- Search across local records.
- Use the app in English or Chinese.

这个 App 主要帮助用户：

- 快速查看重要事项的准备状态。
- 跟踪常见留学生活任务。
- 集中保存重要文件记录和备注。
- 整理旅行计划和旅行材料。
- 简单记录月度生活开销。
- 快速找到紧急联系方式。
- 在本地记录中搜索信息。
- 使用英文或中文界面。

## Project Boundaries / 项目边界

Study Abroad Life Toolkit is a personal mobile app prototype. It should stay focused on local organization and simple user flows.

The project should not be described as a large platform, official service, or finished public product.

海外留学生活助手是一个个人项目和手机端 App 原型，重点是本地信息整理和简单清楚的操作流程。

项目文档中不应把它描述成大型平台、官方服务或已经完成上线的产品。

## Advice And Liability Boundary / 建议与责任边界

The app does not provide official visa, legal, medical, financial, or immigration advice.

It may help users store reminders such as "check visa status" or "save GP phone number", but users should still check official sources, universities, banks, doctors, government websites, or qualified professionals before making decisions.

这个 App 不提供官方签证、法律、医疗、金融或移民建议。

它可以帮助用户保存类似“检查签证状态”或“保存 GP 电话”的提醒，但用户在做重要决定前，仍然应查看官方渠道、学校、银行、医生、政府网站或合格专业人士的信息。

## First Version Scope / 第一版范围

The first version should include planning and later implementation for:

- Home Dashboard.
- Life Checklist.
- Document Wallet.
- Travel Planner / Travel Wallet.
- Budget Tracker.
- Emergency Contacts.
- Search.
- English and Chinese language support.
- Local-only data storage.

第一版计划覆盖：

- 首页总览。
- 生活事项清单。
- 重要文件夹。
- 旅行材料管理。
- 生活预算记录。
- 紧急联系方式。
- 搜索。
- 中英文切换。
- 仅本地保存数据。

First-version work should not include login, cloud sync, AI chat, community features, map search, or professional advice features.

第一版不做登录、云同步、AI 聊天、社区功能、地图搜索，也不做任何专业建议类功能。
